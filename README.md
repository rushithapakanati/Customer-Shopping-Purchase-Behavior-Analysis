\# Shopping Behaviour Analysis - Excel Dashboard



\## 📌 Problem Statement

An e-commerce company wants to understand customer shopping behaviour to improve sales, marketing, and customer retention. The goal is to analyze purchase patterns across demographics, product categories, seasons, and discount strategies using Excel.



\*\*Dataset:\*\* 3,900 customer transactions with fields like Age, Gender, Category, Purchase Amount, Payment Method, Review Rating, Discount Applied, Promo Code, Shipping Type, Previous Purchases.



\## 🔄 Workflow

1\. \*\*Data Cleaning:\*\* Removed duplicates, handled missing values, standardized Category \& Payment Method names, fixed date formats.

2\. \*\*Data Transformation:\*\* Created Age Groups, calculated Total Revenue, Average Order Value, Repeat Customer Rate using PivotTables \& Formulas (`SUMIFS`, `AVERAGEIFS`, `COUNTIFS`).

3\. \*\*Dashboard Creation:\*\* Built interactive Excel dashboard with 9 charts + KPI cards + Slicers (Season, Category, Age Group, Gender).

4\. \*\*Insights Generation:\*\* Analyzed trends to derive actionable business recommendations.

\## 💡 Key Insights

1\. \*\*California leads\*\* in both customer count and revenue – highest demand region.

2\. \*\*Clothing is the top category\*\* by revenue, followed by Accessories.

3\. \*\*Fall season\*\* generates peak sales, Spring is lowest.

4\. \*\*Discounts drive purchases\*\* – majority of orders used a discount.

5\. \*\*Credit Card \& PayPal\*\* are preferred payment methods (>60% combined).

6\. \*\*Standard shipping\*\* is most chosen, but Express has higher AOV.

7\. \*\*Age Group 35-50\*\* are the highest spending customers.



\## ✅ Recommendations

1\. Increase inventory and marketing spend in \*\*California\*\* and for \*\*Clothing\*\* category before Fall season.

2\. Continue \*\*discount \& promo code strategy\*\* – it directly correlates with higher conversion.

3\. Optimize checkout for \*\*Credit Card/PayPal\*\* and offer Express shipping upsell.

4\. Launch loyalty program targeting \*\*repeat customers (97.8%)\*\* to increase AOV.

5\. Run re-engagement campaigns for \*\*low-review categories\*\* to improve 3.75 rating to 4.0+.
